**PowerPlay Team GP Tracker**
Updated every **Monday morning (3 to 4 AM ET)** with games played through Sunday.

**Rule 5.5:** every team needs **1,200 games played** by players in its **20 active slots** (12 F, 6 D, 2 G) over the regular season, Sep 29 to Feb 28. Games by players on the bench or in the minors do not count. Fantasy playoffs do not count.
Missing the floor: a non-playoff team drops to pick 16; a playoff team drops to pick 31. The Cup champion is exempt.

**How to read the table**
`TEAM` official 3-letter team code
`GP  ` games played by players in your active slots so far
`PACE` GP divided by the games those slots could have played (days their NHL clubs played)
`PROJ` projected season total: PACE x 1,680 (20 slots x 84 games)
`DEAD` an active player's club played but he didn't (scratched, AHL, backup goalie)
`AVD ` extra games a better lineup would have counted (bench and minors included)
`!   ` projected below 1,200

Numbers are checked against the Fantrax standings every week.
